ChessBreak Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026. Applies to ChessBreak 0.1.2.

Privacy at a glance

ChessBreak works offline and saves chess progress and preferences locally in your browser. ChessBreak does not receive your gameplay data, track your browsing, or use analytics or advertising. If you contact support or open the public privacy page, the email and hosting providers may process the information described below.

Who is responsible

Finn Vreeman, the independent developer of ChessBreak, is responsible for the developer-controlled processing described in this policy. For support and privacy requests, email VreemanIT@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Chrome extension, optional support emails, and the public copy of this policy when hosted on GitHub Pages. Reading the bundled policy inside the extension does not contact GitHub Pages.

Local chess progress

ChessBreak stores gameplay activity and preferences on your device using chrome.storage.local. This includes the current puzzle and moves, mistakes and hints, solved counts, streaks, practice rating, difficulty, display preferences, last screen, and the current Daily Challenge date, progress, and results.

These records are used only to run the chess puzzles and let you resume your progress. They are not transmitted to the ChessBreak developer or third parties, sold, or used for advertising. Quick Puzzle and Daily Challenge progress are stored separately. Daily results are replaced when the extension detects a new UTC day.

No browsing access or analytics

The extension does not read the websites you visit, browsing history, personal communications, identity, location, passwords, or files. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, cloud sync, or backend. All required code and chess puzzles are bundled with the extension, so normal play works offline without network requests.

The storage permission is used only for local chess progress and preferences. ChessBreak does not use chrome.storage.sync. It does not set advertising or tracking cookies. The standalone privacy page contains no analytics scripts, external fonts, tracking pixels, or contact forms.

Optional support emails

If you email the developer, the developer receives your email address, any name you provide, message contents, attachments, and normal email metadata. These are used to answer your question and handle necessary follow-up, not for mailing lists or advertising. Sending an email is optional and is not required to play. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

The support mailbox is hosted by Google Gmail. Google and the email providers involved handle delivery and storage under their applicable terms and privacy policies. See Google's privacy policy.

Public privacy page and hosting

When you visit a GitHub Pages copy of this page, your browser sends technical request data to GitHub, including your IP address and information needed to request the page. GitHub explicitly states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, including for visitors who are not signed in. No chess progress is sent with these requests. Website hosting is separate from offline play.

See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub General Privacy Statement for GitHub's processing, recipients, and privacy contacts. GitHub describes its own service and security processing as a data controller. This policy does not replace GitHub's statement.

Purposes and lawful bases

ChessBreak stores progress and settings locally to provide the offline game and save your preferences. Where this processing involves personal data and the GDPR applies, the developer relies on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): allowing users to resume puzzles and keep their chosen settings without creating an account or sending data to a server.

For optional support emails, the developer relies on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) to respond to messages, resolve issues, and keep necessary follow-up correspondence. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where specific processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c) applies instead.

GitHub processes technical information associated with visiting the public privacy page for its own hosting, security, and other purposes under its own privacy statement. The developer does not receive GitHub Pages visitor logs. The developer does not use these visits for advertising or analytics.

No personal details are required to play. Local storage is needed to retain your puzzle and settings; if it is unavailable, progress cannot be saved reliably. Your practice rating is calculated automatically from puzzle results for gameplay only. ChessBreak makes no decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Chrome APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ChessBreak uses the Chrome storage API for local progress and preferences only.

Recipients and international processing

Only the developer and the providers needed for support email or website delivery receive the corresponding support/website data. Information may also be disclosed where a specific legal obligation requires it or where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim. Gameplay records remain on your device unless you choose to include them in an email.

Google Gmail and GitHub may process support or website data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Their published transfer information describes adequacy mechanisms, including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where applicable, and standard contractual clauses for relevant transfers. See Google's transfer information and the international-transfers and contact sections of GitHub's privacy statement linked above for details and ways to request information about safeguards. These providers also describe their own processing purposes as independent controllers.

Retention and deletion

Local progress stays in your Chrome profile until it is replaced through normal use or removed. Uninstalling ChessBreak removes its local extension storage. Reinstalling starts with new progress. The developer cannot access or recover your local records.

Support correspondence is deleted no later than six months after the request is resolved, unless a specific legal obligation or an ongoing legal claim requires keeping the relevant records longer. Only the records needed for that obligation or claim are retained for that additional period.

GitHub's general privacy statement describes retention according to processing purposes, contractual and legal requirements, dispute resolution, and enforcement of agreements. Its Pages documentation does not specify a fixed number of days for visitor IP logs. Those logs are retained by GitHub under its own policy; the extension does not keep or upload them. Google describes provider-side retention in its privacy policy linked above. Deleting an email from the support mailbox does not override a provider's separate backup, security, or legally required retention.

Your privacy rights

Where the GDPR applies, you can request access to, correction or deletion of, or restriction of the processing of your personal data. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests, and request data portability where its legal conditions apply. Email VreemanIT@gmail.com to make a request. Requests are normally handled within one month; if a lawful extension is needed, you will be told within that month.

The developer cannot identify you from or remotely access your local chess records. You can view your progress in the extension and remove all local records by uninstalling it. No identity data will be collected solely to identify otherwise unidentifiable local records. Requests about support correspondence or website data will be handled with the relevant providers where necessary.

You may complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands or the competent data protection authority where you live, work, or believe an infringement occurred.

Support button and changes

The coffee-support button is inactive in this release. ChessBreak does not collect or process payment information. If the features, providers, or processing practices change, this policy will be updated. Its date and extension version identify the current text.
